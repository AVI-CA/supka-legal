Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026
App Name: Sea Battle: Local Wireless

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sea Battle: Local Wireless (the “App”) handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, share, or process any personal information, user accounts, analytics, or telemetry data.
  • Local Network Communication: The App uses Apple’s MultipeerConnectivity framework solely to establish a direct peer-to-peer (P2P) connection between devices over local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
  • Data Transmission: All game data (such as move coordinates and game status) is transmitted directly between players’ devices within the local network. No game data or communication is ever sent to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party services.
  • Device names: Nearby device names provided by iOS may appear on the participating devices during a local match. Those names stay on the devices and are not collected or stored by us.

3. Permissions

The App requests access to the Local Network exclusively to discover and connect with nearby devices for local multiplayer gameplay. You can revoke this permission at any time through your iOS device settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data from any users, it is suitable for users of all ages and does not collect information covered by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
