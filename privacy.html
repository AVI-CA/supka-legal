Privacy Policy for SUPKA

Last updated: July 26, 2026

At SUPKA, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile applications on iPhone, iPad, Android, and the optional Apple Watch companion.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Data: We collect precise GPS location on your device to record paddling routes and show your position on the map during an active workout. Full route tracks are stored locally on your phone by default.
  • Account Information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive basic profile information (such as name and email) from that provider.
  • Workout Summaries: If you use SUPKA cloud sync (Pro), we store workout summary metrics (distance, duration, speeds, and similar stats). We do not upload your raw GPS track to our cloud database.
  • Photos: Workout photos you add are stored on your device. Optional cloud photo storage may be offered later; if enabled, that would be disclosed in-app and governed by this policy.
  • Backups you choose: You may back up workouts and settings to iCloud or Google Drive using your own Apple / Google account quota. Those backups are controlled by you and subject to Apple’s or Google’s terms for that storage.
  • Usage / diagnostics: We may process diagnostic information to improve stability (for example via Firebase).

Apple Watch companion

If you install SUPKA on Apple Watch, with your permission the Watch app may use:

  • Heart rate and workout session data via HealthKit / workout APIs during a session
  • Motion sensors to estimate paddle cadence (strokes per minute)
  • Compass / heading to show direction on the workout screen
  • WatchConnectivity to send live metrics (such as heart rate, cadence, energy, and session controls) to the paired iPhone

This Watch data is used to power your live session and the workout saved on your phone. We do not sell health or sensor data.

Weather and water conditions

On iPhone, SUPKA may use your approximate location to show weather and water context:

  • Apple WeatherKit (primary on iOS when available), with Apple’s legal attribution
  • Open-Meteo as a fallback air-weather source
  • Public water / ocean data providers (for example USGS, NOAA) where available

Weather and water information is for convenience only and is not a substitute for official marine forecasts or safety advice.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Provide tracking, mapping, statistics, and Watch companion features on your devices
  • Enable Liveguard: when you choose, SUPKA opens the system share sheet so you can send a Maps location pin via Messages, WhatsApp, or another app you select. Liveguard does not stream your location to a SUPKA live-tracking server
  • Sync Pro cloud summaries when you are signed in and entitled
  • Improve app performance and fix technical issues

3. Soft trial, Pro, and payments

SUPKA may offer a time-limited soft trial of Pro features and optional paid plans (monthly, yearly, or lifetime). Purchases on Apple platforms are processed by Apple; we do not receive your full payment card details. Entitlement status may be stored on device and/or with your account so Pro features work across reinstalls where supported.

4. Data Deletion

You can delete workouts and photos from the app. Cloud summary data associated with your account can be removed when you delete your account or request deletion. Liveguard shares are delivered by the messaging apps you use; SUPKA does not keep a separate live-tracking copy of those shares on our servers. You can revoke HealthKit access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, and remove Watch permissions in watchOS / iPhone Settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data or the deletion of your account and associated data at any time via the app settings or by contacting us.

7. Apple Health Integration

SUPKA optionally integrates with Apple Health (HealthKit).

  • Data: With your permission, we may read and write workout-related data (such as active energy, distance, duration, and heart-rate related workout data) so sessions appear in Apple Health and contribute to Activity Rings where applicable.
  • Control: Revoke access anytime in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices. We do not sell or share your health data with third-party advertisers.
